## Brand story

**Growth teams have never had more room to grow.**

Meta's ranking now decides who sees an ad based on the creative itself. Targeting is no longer a setting. It is the idea, the hook, the format and the person on screen. The brand that finds better ideas faster grows faster.

But we noticed something getting in the way.

The budget, the product and the ambition were all there. What slowed teams down was the work required to know what to run next: reading results, mining reviews, scouting competitors, ranking angles, and writing enough good briefs to keep the account fed. A strategist can do that for eight ads a week. Andromeda wants eighty.

We believe growth teams should never be bottlenecked on ideas.

That is why Yolodex brings the work usually spread across a strategist, an analyst, a researcher and a copywriter into one AI creative strategy team. It takes every winning ad apart frame by frame, isolates which variable drove the result, mines what customers actually say, and turns that into shoot-ready briefs at ten times the pace. Then the ads run, the numbers come back, and the next sprint starts smarter.

So more angles get tested. More briefs get shot. More winners get found. And every result makes the next brief better.

**Yolodex gives growth teams the creative to scale spend without scaling headcount.**

## Tone of voice

Three keywords. Every piece of Yolodex writing should be recognisably all three.

### 1. Evidence-first

**Every claim carries a number, a name, or a mechanism.**

Yolodex earns belief by showing the working. We name the brand, quote the stat, or explain the model. We never assert "better" without saying better than what, by how much, measured how. When we don't have the number yet, we describe the mechanism instead.

### 2. Plain

**We explain the machine in words a head of growth can repeat at a dinner.**

Short sentences. One idea each. No jargon for its own sake, no puns, no cleverness that costs a re-read. If a reader can't picture how Yolodex works from the copy, the copy is wrong, not the reader. Plain is not dumbed down. It is precise.

### 3. Bullish

**We say what we do and what it does, without hedging.**

Yolodex is confident because the results are. We lead with the claim, not with "we think" or "we help". No exclamation marks, no urgency tricks, no ALL CAPS. The confidence comes from specifics and declaratives. We are plain, but never understated.

## House styles

These are the rules we always action. Everything else is judgement.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Pick one spelling convention per piece and keep it consistent. | Assume readers know internal names (persona brain, opportunity matrix). |
| Write in active voice. "Yolodex writes the brief", not "briefs are generated". | Open with a product claim. Open with the reader's situation or the result. |
| Lead with the claim, then the evidence, in that order. | Use unlock, supercharge, revolutionise, game-changing, seamless, leverage, cutting-edge, next-level, or "AI-powered" as an adjective. |
| Put a number, a name, or a mechanism in every claim. | Use exclamation marks or emoji in marketing copy. |
| Spell out zero to nine. Use numerals for 10 and above. Percentages always as numerals (90%). | Write "10x" as a verb. Fine as a stat. |
| Keep paragraphs short. One idea per paragraph. | Write long blocks of uninterrupted copy. |
| Use contractions where they sound natural (we're, it's, you'll). | Write to impress. Write to be understood instead. |
| Name real brands and real people wherever we have permission. | Talk about "brands like yours". Name the brand or describe the number. |
| Say "Yolodex" and "your AI creative strategy team". Agents get capitalised job titles. | Say "the Yolodex", "YOLODEX", or "our AI". |

## Tagline

> ## Creative is the new targeting. We build the creative briefs that win.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Creative is the new targeting.** | A point of view, not a sales claim. It names the Andromeda-era truth a head of growth already half believes, so the reader nods before we've said what we sell. |
| **We build** | Active. Yolodex does the work. It is a team you hire, not a tool you operate. |
| **creative briefs** | The unit of output. The thing a growth team actually ships to production. Concrete, familiar, un-hyped. |
| **that win.** | The outcome. Win rate, not volume. It ties the promise to the number we can prove. |

**Why it works.** It is two sentences that each stand alone. The first is a belief that makes Yolodex necessary. The second is a promise that makes Yolodex specific. Together they explain the category without naming it.

**Shortlist if the two-line version is too long for a placement**

1. Know what to run next.
2. Never be bottlenecked on what ads to run.
3. 10x the briefs. Same team. Higher win rate.

## Category name

> ## AI creative strategy team

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI** | How. Signals speed, scale and learning. Universally recognised, no explanation needed. |
| **creative strategy** | The function. This is the layer that bottlenecks (the brief, not the production), and the layer Motion, Parker and the DIY stack fail to automate. |
| **team** | Scale and shape. Not one assistant: seven agents with real jobs and a lead. "Team" is the word a head of growth uses about the thing they can't hire fast enough. |

**Why it works.** It is simple, factual and says what Yolodex replaces. Nobody in the set can claim "team" without contradicting their single-assistant framing. It also carries the explainer inside it: an AI agent for creative briefs is what a creative strategy team produces.

**The explainer** (use in any context with three seconds and no prior knowledge): *An AI agent for creative briefs that learns from your ad results.*

**Alternatives if needed**

- Creative strategy agent (agent-forward, easy to shorten to "the agent")
- Your AI creative department (warmer, founder-friendly, implies the whole function)
- Agentic creative strategy (names the discipline rather than the product)
