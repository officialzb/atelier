## 1. What Yolodex is

**Category.** Yolodex is an AI creative strategy team.

**Explainer.** An AI agent for creative briefs that learns from your ad results. Use this sentence any time someone has three seconds and no context.

**Promise.** Creative is the new targeting. We build the creative briefs that win.

**Who it's for.** Heads of growth at DTC brands spending $50k to $1M+ a month on Meta. They care about scaling spend without scaling headcount, CAC and win rate, testing velocity, and never running out of winners. Daily users are creative strategists, creative leads, and agency strategists serving several brands.

**What it replaces.** The generic-AI stack (Claude/ChatGPT + Motion/Foreplay), the agency briefing bottleneck, and the strategist hire who still can't keep up.

**Why now.** Meta's Andromeda-era ranking has made creative the targeting. The brand that finds better ideas faster grows faster. Production is no longer the bottleneck. Knowing what to run is.

## 2. Personality and voice

The full verbal identity (brand story, three tone-of-voice keywords with Yes/No/Reasoning tables, house styles, annotated tagline and category name) is in `docs/brand/yolodex-verbal-identity.md`. This section is the summary.

**Archetype: the Scientist.** Rigorous, curious, bullish, plain-spoken, transparent. Yolodex runs the experiment rather than guessing with taste, and it says so without hedging.

**Register.** British, plain, very confident. Not understated. Yolodex states what it does and what the numbers are.

### We are / we are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Evidence-led | Opinionated without proof |
| Plain | Clever for its own sake |
| Confident | Hype-y |
| Specific | Vague |

### Writing rules

1. Lead with the claim, then the evidence. Never the other way round.
2. Every claim carries a number, a name, or a mechanism.
3. Short sentences. One idea each.
4. No exclamation marks in marketing copy. Confidence comes from specifics, not punctuation.
5. British spelling: optimise, colour, programme.
6. Contractions are fine. "We" and "you".
7. Name real brands and real people wherever we have permission.
8. Explain the machine in plain words. If a reader can't picture how it works, rewrite.

### Words

**Use:** briefs, winners, winning ads, angles, personas, evidence, the loop, sprint, shoot-ready, win rate, read-back, what to run next.

**Ban:** unlock, supercharge, revolutionise, game-changing, seamless, leverage, "AI-powered" as an adjective, cutting-edge, next-level, "10x" as a verb (fine as a stat), sparkle emoji.

## 3. The team

Yolodex is a team, not a chatbot. Every agent maps to a real job on an in-house creative strategy team so a head of growth instantly understands what it replaces or multiplies.

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Yolo Badger** (team lead) | Ranks where to play, decides what runs next, hands out the work, reports back in plain English. The in-app voice and the brand mascot. |
| Creative Analyst | Takes every ad apart frame by frame and tags hook, format, pacing, talent, music, claim. |
| Performance Analyst | Reads Meta results and isolates which variables actually drove the win. |
| Insights Researcher | Mines reviews, Reddit, ad comments and surveys for the language customers actually use. |
| Creative Researcher | Scouts competitor and category ads and builds the reference board for each brief. |
| Creative Strategist | Turns persona × angle × evidence into a shoot-ready brief. |
| Copywriter | Writes the hooks, scripts and on-screen text for every brief. |
| Experimentation Lead | Designs the sprint test plan and closes the loop when the ads go live. |

**The mascot.** Yolo Badger is the brand mascot as well as the team lead: the face of Yolodex in-app, on social, in the deck, at events, and the favicon. When Yolodex speaks in first person, it is the badger speaking. Treat the character as a colleague with a job, not a decoration.

**The graphic.** An org chart drawn like a newspaper masthead: Yolo Badger on top, seven bylines beneath, one line each. Used on the homepage, the deck and `/brand`.

## 4. Naming

- Always "Yolodex". Never "the Yolodex" or "YOLODEX" in running text. Wordmark is lowercase "yolodex".
- First mention in any new context: "Yolodex, your AI creative strategy team". Then just "Yolodex".
- Agents are capitalised job titles. The team lead is Yolo Badger.
- Product nouns to keep: briefs, personas, angles, sprints, patterns, the loop.
- Retire from customer-facing copy: "Creative Scientist™" (internal nickname only), "persona brain", "opportunity matrix" (say "where to play").

## 5. Visual identity

### The line

Editorial, pushed hard away from the competitor set. The AI-creative-tool pack is dark-mode SaaS with purple, blue and green gradients. Yolodex looks like the research desk of a serious publication: paper ground, ink type, one signal colour, and real ads with the machine's markup on them.

### Signature device: annotated ads

Real customer ads with Yolodex's markup over them: tags, hairline callouts, variable labels and evidence notes in mono type and the signal colour. This is "show the machine" made visual. No competitor shows their working on the ad itself. If an asset has room for one image, it is an annotated ad.

### Logo and the badger

Two-part system.

1. **The mark: Yolo Badger's head.** Front-on, round wire glasses, cream face with charcoal stripes. This is the favicon, app icon and Slack icon from day one. The glasses are the recognition feature at small sizes and carry the Scientist archetype; keep them in every rendition.
2. **The wordmark:** lowercase "yolodex" in the body grotesk, tight tracking. Lock-up is mark left, wordmark right. The wordmark stands alone on long-form pages; the mark stands alone at small sizes.

**Yolo Badger** is an existing character: chubby, upright, round wire glasses, cream face and belly, charcoal coat with a cream stripe over the head, pink nose, thick eyebrows. Warm and a bit bookish. Three renditions:

| Rendition | Use |
|---|---|
| Full character (colour, soft cartoon line) | In-app moments, empty states, onboarding, the team masthead, social |
| Head mark (simplified, two flat colours: ink + paper, optional signal-colour circle behind) | Favicon 16/32/48px, app icon, Slack, OG corner |
| Line version (single-weight ink outline, no fill) | Editorial pages, print, stamps and footers |

Rules: never stretch, recolour or add gradients. The character is the team lead, so it speaks in first person in-app. It never appears inside customer case-study imagery.

The reference image lives at `public/brand/yolo-badger-reference.png` (drop the original there; the image script uses it as the reference for the mark and masthead prompts).

### Colour

Everything is paper, ink and greys plus one signal colour. The signal is used like a highlighter: underline swipes, tags, one hero block. Never more than about 10% of a composition.

Four palettes are built into `/brand` to compare. Marker yellow is the recommendation.

| Palette | Paper | Ink | Signal | Text on signal | Muted | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marker yellow** (recommended) | `#F7F4EC` | `#111111` | `#FFE600` | `#111111` | `#6B6760` | `#D9D4C7` |
| Salmon paper | `#F5DCC8` | `#1A1A1A` | `#1A1A1A` | `#F5DCC8` | `#6E5A4E` | `#D9B9A3` |
| Oxblood | `#F5F1E8` | `#141414` | `#7A1F2B` | `#FFFFFF` | `#6B6660` | `#D8D2C4` |
| Cobalt | `#F4F2EB` | `#101010` | `#0033CC` | `#FFFFFF` | `#66645E` | `#D7D3C8` |

Rules:
- Body text is always ink on paper (contrast above 15:1 in every palette).
- Yellow is a highlight, never a text colour. Set ink on yellow, not yellow on paper.
- Muted grey is for captions and metadata only, at 0.875rem or larger (contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in every palette).
- A dark "machine" section (ink ground, paper text) is allowed for product screenshots and diagrams only.

### Type

Three Google Fonts pairings are built into `/brand` to compare. Instrument is the recommendation.

| Pairing | Display | Body / UI | Mono (annotations, data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instrument** (recommended) | Instrument Serif | Inter Tight | JetBrains Mono |
| Fraunces | Fraunces | Inter | IBM Plex Mono |
| Newsreader | Newsreader | Instrument Sans | Space Mono |

Scale:

| Token | Size / line-height / tracking |
|---|---|
| display-xl | clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem) / 0.95 / -0.02em |
| display | clamp(2rem, 4.5vw, 3.5rem) / 1.0 / -0.015em |
| h2 | 1.75rem / 1.15 |
| h3 | 1.25rem / 1.3 |
| body | 1.0625rem / 1.5 |
| small | 0.875rem / 1.45 |
| mono-caption | 0.75rem / 1.4 / 0.02em, uppercase |

Numbers are set in the display serif. Sources go underneath in mono-caption.

### Layout

- Generous margins, hairline rules, a real column grid.
- Big serif display, small mono captions with the source under every number.
- Light by default.
- Signal colour at most ~10% of any composition.

### Imagery

- Real customer ads, always annotated. Never a naked screenshot.
- Real product and packaging photography from customers, with permission.
- Diagrams: single-weight line drawings, ink on paper, mono labels.
- Illustration: Yolo Badger (three renditions above) and single-weight technical diagrams. Nothing else.
- No stock photography of any kind.

### Never

Gradients. Blobs. Sparkles or stars. Purple. Dark mode by default. Stock photos of people at laptops. 3D renders. Glassmorphism. Rounded-pill everything.
