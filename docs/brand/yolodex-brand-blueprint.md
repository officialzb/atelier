## Positioning statement

For heads of growth at DTC brands spending $50k to $1M+ a month on Meta, who are bottlenecked on knowing what ads to run next, Yolodex is the AI creative strategy team that finds the winning angles and builds the shoot-ready briefs, then learns from every ad result. Unlike a generic-AI stack, an agency, or another strategist hire, Yolodex is purpose-built for creative strategy and closes the loop from brief to live ad to read-back.

## Why this frame

Meta's Andromeda-era ranking has made creative the targeting. The brand that finds better ideas faster grows faster. Production is no longer the bottleneck; knowing what to run is. Yolodex sells the answer to "what do we run next", at ten times the volume a human team can brief, with a higher win rate because every result feeds the next brief.

## Tagline shortlist

1. Creative is the new targeting. We build the creative briefs that win.
2. Know what to run next.
3. Never be bottlenecked on what ads to run.
4. 10x the briefs. Same team. Higher win rate.
5. Your AI creative strategy team.

## Open decisions

- Signal colour: marker yellow first; salmon paper, oxblood and cobalt are built into the `/brand` page to compare.
- Type pairing: Instrument Serif + Inter Tight + JetBrains Mono recommended; Fraunces and Newsreader pairings are switchable on `/brand`.
- Yolo Badger: existing character (round glasses, cream face, charcoal coat) is the mark and favicon from day one. Head mark to be cut from it; character sheet is phase 2.
