# Brand interview: question bank and answers

This is the "quiz" that produced the blueprint. It can be re-run for another brand by replacing the pre-filled options. Answers below are Zaeem's, 9 Sep 2026.

## Round 1: category and positioning

**Q. Which category frame do you want to own?**
Options: AI agent for creative briefs / Creative Scientist™ / Closed-loop creative system / Creative intelligence for DTC.
A. Liked "AI agent for creative briefs" for clarity, but worried it's a feature not a category. Resolved in round 3 as "AI creative strategy team" with the agent line as the explainer.

**Q. Who is the enemy?**
A. Smaller brands: guessing with taste. Bigger brands: the generic-AI stack (Claude + Motion).

**Q. Keep "Never run out of ads that work"?**
A. No. Wanted lines about scaling the creative-strategy team and the creative wall. Reframed in round 2 around the head of growth and Andromeda: creative is the targeting, find the best ideas, don't get bottlenecked on what to run, reduce time briefing.

**Q. Ambition?**
A. Category creator, anti-SaaS.

## Round 2: promise, audience, story

**Q. Promise line?**
Chosen and edited: "Creative is the new targeting. We build the creative briefs that win."

**Q. Primary buyer?**
A. Head of Growth. Cares about the founder's things (growth, headcount, not running out of winners) plus metrics.

**Q. Hero story?**
A. Peachies. Ideal quote: "We 10x'd our briefing output without increasing workload on creative strategists, whilst improving our win rate. Yolodex briefs now account for 90% of our winning ads."

**Q. What has the buyer tried before?**
A. Claude/ChatGPT + Motion/Foreplay; an agency or freelance strategist; hiring a creative strategist.

**Q. What makes a head of growth trust you?**
A. Show the machine.

## Round 3: category name, personality, voice

**Q. Category name?**
Rejected: creative discovery, creative search, growth creative engine, creative decisioning, creative science ("engines aren't categories"). Chosen: **AI creative strategy team**, with a graphic of the agents inside the team, each mapped to a real marketing job.

**Q. Archetype?** A. The Scientist.

**Q. Register?** A. Plain, confident, not understated. Very confident and bullish. (Later note: no nationality in the voice; spelling just has to be consistent.)

**Q. We are / we are not?** A. Evidence-led not opinionated. Plain not clever.

## Round 4: team, colour, device, type

**Q. Agent roster (real-job names)?**
A. Confirm the seven: Creative Analyst, Performance Analyst, Insights Researcher, Creative Researcher, Creative Strategist, Copywriter, Experimentation Lead. Team lead is **Yolo Badger**.

**Q. Visual line?**
A. Editorial, but very visually different from the competitor set.

**Q. Signal colour?**
A. Try marker yellow first. Build a few alternatives into the output to test (salmon paper, oxblood, cobalt).

**Q. Ownable visual device?** A. Annotated ads.

**Q. Logo and avoid list?**
A. Wordmark first. Avoid gradients, blobs, sparkles, purple, and stock photos of people at laptops.

**Q. Type pairing?**
A. Show a few options in the output (Instrument, Fraunces, Newsreader pairings on `/brand`).

## Re-running this for another brand

Keep the round structure. Replace the pre-filled options with research from the brand's site, sales calls and internal chat, then ask the same questions. Write the answers into a copy of `brand.json` and regenerate the blueprint and guidelines from it.
